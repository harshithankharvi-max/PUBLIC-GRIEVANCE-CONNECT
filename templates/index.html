How the Rural Grievance System Works

A simple, transparent 4-step process connecting rural villagers with administration.

01
🎙️

Report Issue

Villagers submit grievances through voice recordings, text, or location photos. No complex paperwork.

02
🤖

AI Classification

Machine Learning algorithms automatically categorize the problem (Street Lights, Water, Roads, Sanitation) and score its urgency.

03
📍

Geospatial Tagging

GIS coordinates pin the exact rural location on interactive maps for Gram Panchayat and Taluk officers.

04
✅

Swift Resolution

Assigned authorities verify, act, and resolve the grievance with live transparent status updates.

Key Capabilities

Built Specifically for Rural Needs

Designed to overcome digital and literacy barriers in rural communities.

🤖

Automatic AI Categorization

Citizens do not need to guess government departments. Natural Language Processing identifies whether an issue belongs to Electricity, Water & Sanitation, Infrastructure, or Health.

🗺️

Interactive GIS Mapping

Geospatial analysis enables authorities to visualize clusters of problems across villages, ensuring maintenance teams are routed efficiently.

🎙️

Voice-Based Grievance Reporting

Enables illiterate and elderly rural citizens to easily report grievances by simply speaking into their mobile device or village kiosk.

📸

Geotagged Photo Evidence

Citizens can upload photos of broken water pipes, damaged culverts, or dark streets with auto-captured GPS coordinates.

🌐

Bilingual (Kannada & English)

Complete dual-language support enables seamless accessibility in regional Kannada alongside English.

🛡️

Secure Role-Based Portals

Strict segregation between Citizen and Administrative views. Dedicated administrator tools with priority filtering and status controls.
